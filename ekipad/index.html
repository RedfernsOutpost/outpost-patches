Ekipad

Your iPhone as a game controller for your Mac. Bluetooth, peer-to-peer Wi-Fi or USB, with no router, no account and nothing sent to the internet.

Ekipad is the iPhone app. It works with Ekipad Host, a free app for your Mac that turns what you press into a game controller, keyboard keys or mouse movement.

Questions

What do I need?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later and a Mac with macOS 14 or later running Ekipad Host. The virtual game controller needs macOS 15 or later; on macOS 14 the keyboard and mouse routes work.

How do I get Ekipad Host onto my Mac?

Download it from the link above, unzip it, move Ekipad Host to Applications and open it. From the phone, Settings › Mac app › Send Mac app link sends the download link to your Mac with AirDrop, Messages or Mail.

How do I pair my phone?

Open Ekipad Host on the Mac and Ekipad on the iPhone. Then either scan the QR code on the Mac's Gate page (Ekipad › Settings › Scan QR, or the iPhone Camera), tap the four pictograms shown on the Mac into Settings › Pictogram Code, or just click Allow when the phone appears at the Gate. After that the phone connects by itself.

What does the Mac ask permission for?

Bluetooth and Local Network, to reach your phone. Accessibility (Key Output), to press keys, move the mouse and create the virtual controller. Screen Recording, only if you turn on Screen Share. Ekipad Host explains each one before macOS asks.

A permission is on in System Settings, but Ekipad Host says it's off

This can happen after replacing or moving the app. In Ekipad Host click Allow again and choose Reset and Allow Again, then switch it back on in System Settings. Needs Ekipad Host 1.2 or later.

My game doesn't see the controller

On the Mac's Platform page, open your phone's Route menu and choose Xbox Controller (virtual), under Game controller. The routes under Keyboard & mouse type keys instead, for games without controller support.

The left stick types W, A, S, D

Your phone is on a keyboard route. Pick Xbox Controller (virtual) in its Route menu, or use Auto-switch by game to change the route only while a particular game is in front.

The phone can't find my Mac

Make sure Ekipad Host is open on the Mac, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are on on both devices (they don't need to join a network) and Ekipad is allowed Bluetooth and Local Network in the iPhone's Settings › Apps › Ekipad. A USB cable to the Mac also works.

Which connection does it use?

With Link set to Auto, Ekipad sends over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi together and the Mac keeps whichever copy arrives first. With a USB cable plugged in, the controls go over the cable and Bluetooth and Wi-Fi wait on standby (STBY) to save battery, ready to take over the moment you unplug.

Can I use the phone as a mouse?

Yes. Tap Mouse at the top of the controller: the screen becomes a trackpad with big Left and Right click buttons and a scroll strip. Drag to move, tap to click, two-finger tap to right-click and two fingers to scroll.

How does Screen mode work?

Turn on Screen Share in Ekipad Host, then tap Screen on the phone. Your Mac's display appears on the phone and it switches to the Mouse layout. Tap Pad to put the controller over the picture instead.

It says to update one of the apps

The phone and the Mac speak different versions. Update Ekipad from the App Store, or Ekipad Host from its menu bar menu › Check for Updates. When the Mac app is the older one, the phone offers to send the update link to your Mac.

How do I update Ekipad Host?

From version 1.2, when a new version is out Ekipad Host shows a banner: click Install and Relaunch. It downloads the update, checks it's signed by Redfern's Outpost and notarized by Apple, and restarts with it, keeping your pairings, routes and permissions. Your phone also tells you when an update is waiting. On 1.0 or 1.1, download the latest version once from the link above and replace the old app in Applications.

Does Ekipad collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and everything travels directly between your own phone and Mac. The only internet requests are Ekipad Host checking GitHub for a newer version, which you can turn off, and downloading it from GitHub when you click Install. Details are in the privacy policy.
