Tinbox

A pocket tin full of games. GBA, GB and GBC on your iPhone, with no ads, no accounts and no paid version.

Comes with two free homebrew games, so there's something to play the moment the lid opens.

Questions

How do I add my own games?

Tap Import ROM in the Library and pick a .gba, .gb, .gbc or zipped file from the Files app. Tinbox plays legally obtained game files; it doesn't download games or link to them.

Where are my games and saves?

In the Files app, under On My iPhone › Tinbox: ROMs, Saves, States, Covers and more. Nothing is hidden or uploaded.

How do I back everything up?

Settings › Library › Back up saves and states, or copy the whole Tinbox folder out of the Files app.

Can I delete the included games?

Yes. Long-press one in the Library and delete it. It won't come back.

How do save states, rewind and the Time Capsule work?

Press MENU while playing to open the Quick Menu. From there you can save and load states, rewind the last few seconds, toggle fast-forward, or open the Time Capsule to scrub back through automatic snapshots of your session.

Does the speedrun timer count against a personal best?

Yes. Turn it on in the Quick Menu. It keeps your best time for each game and shows how far ahead or behind you are.

What does signing in to RetroAchievements send?

Only what unlocking needs: a fingerprint of each game you open, the achievements you earn, and a short "now playing" status while a game runs. Nothing is sent while you are signed out. Details are in the privacy policy.

Do Bluetooth controllers work?

Yes. Pair any MFi or standard Bluetooth controller with your iPhone. Remap buttons under Settings › Controls.

Does Tinbox collect any data?

No. No accounts, ads or analytics. Tinbox only goes online for optional box art and, if you sign in, RetroAchievements.
