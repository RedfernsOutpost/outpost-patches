Ekipad: Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

This policy covers Ekipad for iPhone and Ekipad Host for Mac, by Redfern's Outpost. Ekipad does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no servers. Everything it does happens on your iPhone and your own Mac.

What Ekipad uses, and why

  • Bluetooth, to send controller input from your iPhone to your Mac, directly between your two devices.
  • Local network and peer-to-peer Wi-Fi, to find your Mac and send input and the screen picture with low delay, directly between your two devices.
  • USB cable, when plugged in: the same, over the cable.
  • Camera (iPhone, only when you tap Scan QR), to read the pairing code shown on your Mac. Processed on the phone; no images are saved or sent.
  • Motion sensors (iPhone, only if you turn on Gyro), to aim by tilting the phone. Sent only to your paired Mac, as controller input.
  • Accessibility (Mac), to press the keys and move the mouse your controller is mapped to, and to create the virtual game controller. Stays on your Mac.
  • Screen Recording (Mac, only if you turn on Screen Share), to show your Mac's screen on your paired phone, over your local connection only.

Update check

About once a day, Ekipad Host (the version downloaded from GitHub) reads the public list of releases on GitHub to see whether a newer version exists. It sends nothing about you; like any web request, GitHub sees your IP address and the app's version, and GitHub's own privacy statement applies. Turn it off in the menu bar: Check for Updates Automatically. When you click Install and Relaunch, Ekipad Host downloads the new version from the same GitHub release. The iPhone app makes no internet requests.

What's stored on your devices

  • Your settings, button layouts and routes.
  • A cryptographic key pair for each device, kept in the Keychain and used to pair your phone and Mac securely. Your phone remembers your Mac's public key, and your Mac remembers which phones you've allowed. Your device names appear in each other's apps so you can tell them apart.

This never leaves your devices except between your own paired phone and Mac. Deleting the app removes it; the Keychain key may remain until you reset the device or remove it in Keychain Access.

What Ekipad does not do

  • No data is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive data from the app.
  • No tracking, no advertising identifiers, no third-party SDKs, no crash or analytics reporting.
  • No data is sold or shared.

Children

Ekipad is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with the date above updated. Because Ekipad collects no data, a change could never apply to data we hold: we hold none.
